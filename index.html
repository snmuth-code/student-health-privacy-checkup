Three places to look

Fitness & wellness apps

These apps may track things like activity, sleep, or location. Knowing what an app collects helps you decide what to share.

Questions to ask

  • What information does this app collect, and is any of it optional?
  • Does it need my location, contacts, or photos to work?
  • Can I use it without linking other apps or accounts?

Period trackers

Cycle data can be personal. Check what the app stores, who it may be shared with, and what controls you have.

Questions to ask

  • Which details are required, and which can I leave out?
  • Does the app say whether data is shared with other companies?
  • Can I export or delete my data, and how?

Telehealth portals

Portals can hold messages, appointments, and visit information. Check how you sign in and who can see your account.

Questions to ask

  • Who runs this portal: my provider, my school, or another company?
  • What sign-in protections are available, such as two-step verification?
  • Who do I contact with privacy questions about my account?
